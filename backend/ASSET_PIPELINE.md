# Photo asset pipeline

## Implemented

- Accessory recipes shared by Blender and fallback GLBs: laptop, keyboard,
  mouse, cup, bottle, vase, door, blinds, curtain rod, ceiling fan, picture frame,
  desk lamp and cushion. Models are regenerated by AssetLibrary.ensure_generated.
- Bed construction variants (wood/default, upholstered, metal) and distinct
  armchair geometry. Variants are visual approximations, not exact asset retrieval.
- Desktop support association rejects distant or oversized objects and reports
  a support warning rather than teleporting them onto the nearest desk.
- `audit_saved_rooms.py` runs read-only support checks on distinct saved rooms.
  It does not constitute a visual cross-photo benchmark or recognition test.

- Dedicated Blender geometry: bookshelf, upright/flat book, wardrobe, drawer dresser,
  pleated curtain, leafy potted plant, monitor with stand, rug, wall shelf.
- Reusable fallback GLBs: `assets/models/`; inventory: `assets/asset_manifest.json`.
- Books snap to actual generated shelf panel heights; monitors attach to desktops;
  curtain panels attach in front of their image-associated window; plants remain
  on the floor unless image evidence connects their base to a tabletop.
- Photo-region colors: bedding, curtains, rugs, books, storage furniture. These
  are sampled appearance colors, not recovered intrinsic material albedo.
- Preserve existing bed/blanket and desk/chair regression tests. Never add unseen
  books, curtains or other furnishings to uploaded rooms. Unknown labels retain
  an explicit generic volume; the catalog is not exhaustive.

## Verification and remaining work

Run `python -m unittest test_asset_expansion test_blanket test_projection_fit
test_wall_contacts test_cloth_color test_placement_regressions test_gemini_scene`.
Frontend: `npm test`, then `npm run build`.

Geometry export and deterministic support tests do not prove recognition or
placement across arbitrary photos. Multi-photo live validation and a browser
WASD/mouse/mobile visual check remain necessary. No supplied source photo is a
default scene or an inference lookup key.

Window height remains unresolved: fitting the existing camera reproduces its
current position. Camera pose/height calibration must be validated against floor
and wall evidence before changing window elevation. Do not add a fixed offset.
